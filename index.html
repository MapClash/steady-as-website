A nautical instrument on your watch face, every day.

Course and speed when you're underway, your last trip and the way home when you're not. An Apple Watch app for motorboats, sloops, RIBs, tenders and sailboats.

What it does

  • On your watch face: course, speed, trip, log, light and the way home, from the watch's own GPS. Every trip feeds it.
  • Built for every boat: a Motor and a Sail profile, in knots, km/h or mph.
  • Safety in the same instrument: Man Overboard, Hold course and speed limit alerts.
  • Made for Apple Watch Ultra: Action button, Water Lock, no iPhone needed on board.

No account. No data collected. Works offline.
