The instrument beside the helm.

Course and speed on your Apple Watch face, every trip in the logbook on your iPhone. Each works on its own, together they're better. For motorboats, sloops, RIBs, tenders and sailboats, next to whatever you already steer by.

What it does

  • On your wrist: a real instrument on your watch face. Course, speed, trip, log, light and the way home, from the watch's own GPS. Ashore it shows days ashore and the bearing home.
  • On your iPhone: the whole logbook. Every trip on a map, sea miles against famous sea routes, your wake, your season and 26 signal flags to hoist.
  • Each on its own: the watch needs no iPhone aboard, the iPhone needs no watch. Use both and every trip lands in one logbook.
  • Not another navigation app: it sits beside your compass, plotter or chart app, never instead of them.
  • Built for every boat: a Motor and a Sail profile, in knots, km/h or mph.
  • Safety in the same glance: Man Overboard, Hold course and speed limit alerts.

No account. No data collected. Works offline.
