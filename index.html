Boat speed and course on your wrist.

A nautical instrument for Apple Watch. Motorboats, sloops, RIBs, tenders and sailboats.

What it does

  • Course and speed over ground from the watch's own GPS, right on your watch face.
  • Man Overboard: mark the spot and get an arrow back to it.
  • Hold course and speed limit alerts, so you don't have to keep checking.
  • A log of every trip, with the route on your iPhone.

No account. No data collected. Works offline.
