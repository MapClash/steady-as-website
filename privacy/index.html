Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Steady As does not collect any data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything stays on your devices.

Location

Steady As uses your Apple Watch's location to show your course and speed and to record your trips. Location is processed on your devices only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your trips

Trips are stored on your Apple Watch. If you use the iPhone app, trips and settings are synced directly between your own watch and iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.

Apple Health

If you use the Sail profile or turn on logging to Health, Steady As saves sailing workouts and their routes to Apple Health, with your permission. Steady As does not read any data from Health. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Exporting

When you choose to export a trip as a GPX file, it goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

Third parties

Steady As contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Maps in the iPhone app are provided by Apple Maps. Apple's own services, such as Health and iCloud backup, are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Steady As collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
