Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The Steady As app sends nothing to us unless you turn on Fleet total. We have no accounts and no ads. Everything stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. What leaves your device: a few positions from each trip, sent to Apple after the trip for its weather and place names, and, only if you turn on Fleet total, a short note per trip under a random ID, with no position in it.

Location

Steady As uses the location of your Apple Watch, or of your iPhone when you run a trip there, to show your course and speed and to record your trips. The iPhone uses location only while a trip runs, including in the background and on the Lock Screen. Location is processed on your devices. It is never sent to us. The one place it leaves your device is the trip weather and place names, described below.

Your trips

Trips are stored on the device that recorded them. If you use both the watch and the iPhone app, trips and settings are synced directly between your own watch and iPhone.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the iPhone app keeps your trips, settings, and fuel and service entries in your own private iCloud storage, so a new iPhone or a new watch gets them back. This data is stored by Apple in your account. We cannot see it and it never reaches us. You can turn iCloud sync off in the app's Settings, and remove the stored data in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Deleting the app removes the trips on that device. Trips kept in iCloud stay there until you remove them.

Fuel and service

From version 1.2 you can add fuel fills and engine services to your logbook on the iPhone. A service can carry photos, scans and PDFs of its report or invoice. You choose each one: a scan with the camera, a photo you pick, or a file. The camera is used only while you scan, with your permission, and only the scan you keep is saved. Steady As sees only the photos you pick, not your library. Entries and their files are stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, like your trips. They are never sent to us and never to your watch.

Trip weather and place names

To show the weather on a trip in your log, Steady As asks Apple Weather once, after the trip, for the hours the trip ran. The watch sends the position where the trip started and its start hour. The iPhone also sends one position for each stretch of the trip (a new one about every 15 km) with the hours spent there. To name where a trip started, ended and, for a trip that comes back where it started, where it went, the iPhone asks Apple Maps once for the place at those positions: the start, the end and the trip's longest stop or furthest point. It may also ask Apple Maps for an island near one of those positions, and for the center of the town it is in. Only these positions and times are sent, to Apple, and never to us. The answers are kept on your device. Apple Weather and Apple Maps are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Health

If you use the Sail profile or turn on logging to Health, Steady As saves sailing workouts and their routes to Apple Health, with your permission. Steady As does not read any data from Health. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

While a sailing workout runs on your watch, Apple Health shares it with the Steady As app on your own iPhone, so the trip can show on your iPhone's Lock Screen. This stays between your watch and your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the iPhone app can show one notice when a trip starts on your watch, so you can put the trip on your Lock Screen with one tap. Every notice is made on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off in your iPhone's Settings, under Notifications.

Exporting

When you choose to export a trip as a GPX file, or all your trips as a Steady As file (with your fuel and service entries, without their files), it goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

Fleet total (optional)

From version 1.1 you can add your trips to the miles all Steady As boaters sail together. It is off until you turn it on, in Settings or when the iPhone app asks once after your first trip. With it on, the device that recorded a trip sends one note per trip after you moor: the distance in nautical miles, the duration in minutes, the boat type (Motor or Sail), whether you used Mark, hold course and a speed limit on that trip, whether it ran on the watch or the iPhone, the app version, the day the trip ended (no time of day) and a random fleet ID. Settings shows the last note under What is sent.

It never contains your position, your track, your home port, start or end times, speeds, your boat's name, your Apple ID, your name or your device. The fleet ID is made on your device when you turn Fleet total on and is not linked to you. Turning it off deletes the ID and any notes not yet sent; turning it on again makes a new ID.

The notes go to PostHog, our analytics provider, on its servers in the European Union. PostHog is told not to look up or keep your IP address or location. We use the notes only to count the fleet's trips and miles and to see which features boaters use, never for advertising or tracking, and we do not share them.

This website

The website steadyas.app has no analytics, no ads and no cookies. Before Steady As was in the App Store, you could leave your email address on the site to hear when it launched. That form is closed. We use the addresses only for that one note, never share them, and delete them once it is sent. The form was handled by our web host, Netlify, which holds the address and the button you came from until then. To have your address removed sooner, write to us at the address below.

Third parties

Steady As contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. Fleet total sends its notes to PostHog with a plain web request, only when you turn it on. Maps in the iPhone app are provided by Apple Maps. Apple's own services, such as Health and iCloud backup, are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The Steady As app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Fleet total, when turned on, sends only the trip notes described above, under a random ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
