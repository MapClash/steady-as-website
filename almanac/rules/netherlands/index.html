Dutch waters

BPR, RPR, Westerschelde, Eems and the sea: the official Dutch rules.

The official texts. Which one applies depends on where you sail.

  • BPR Most Dutch inland waters.
  • RPR The Rhine in the Netherlands, with the Waal and the Lek.
  • Westerschelde The Westerschelde and its mouths.
  • Eemsmonding The Eems mouth, shared with Germany.
  • BvA At sea: the COLREGs, in their official Dutch translation.

From the Almanac in Steady As, the instrument beside the helm for Apple Watch and iPhone. Steady As is not a government body. Check the official source before you rely on a rule.
