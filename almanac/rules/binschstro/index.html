Binnenschifffahrtsstraßen-Ordnung

BinSchStrO · German federal inland waterways.

The Binnenschifffahrtsstraßen-Ordnung, the rules on Germany's federal inland waterways, as published on 6 May 2026. The official text, in German. Not the Rhine (RheinSchPV), the Mosel, the Danube or the sea lanes.

§ 1.11: small craft (Kleinfahrzeuge) need not carry a copy; it is here for reference.

Official text: gesetze-im-internet.de ›
