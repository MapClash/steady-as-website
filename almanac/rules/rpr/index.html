Rijnvaartpolitiereglement 1995

RPR · The Rhine in the Netherlands, with the Waal and the Lek.

The Rijnvaartpolitiereglement 1995, the rules on the Rhine in the Netherlands, with the Waal and the Lek, in force from 17 June 2026. The official text, in Dutch. The same rules as the German RheinSchPV, in Dutch.

Article 1.11: an up-to-date copy on board, on paper or a screen, except on small craft and push barges.

Official text: wetten.overheid.nl ›
