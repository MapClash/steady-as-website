Official rulebooks

The rules of the road on the water, as each country publishes them, article by article. Which one applies depends on where you sail.

Dutch waters

BPR, RPR, Westerschelde, Eems and the sea: the official Dutch rules.

  • BPR Most Dutch inland waters.
  • RPR The Rhine in the Netherlands, with the Waal and the Lek.
  • Westerschelde The Westerschelde and its mouths.
  • Eemsmonding The Eems mouth, shared with Germany.
  • BvA At sea: the COLREGs, in their official Dutch translation.

German waters

BinSchStrO and the Rhine: the official German rules.

  • BinSchStrO German federal inland waterways.
  • RheinSchPV The Rhine from Basel to the sea.

French waters

RIPAM, the rules at sea in French: where to get the official text.

  • RIPAM At sea: the COLREGs in French, from SHOM.

US waters

Inland Navigation Rules: the official US text.

  • Inland Rules US inland waters, inside the COLREGS demarcation lines.

Canadian waters

Collision Regulations, in English and French: the official Canadian text.

  • Collision Regulations Canadian waters, in English: the International Rules with Canada's own changes.
  • Règlement sur les abordages The same rules, in French.

New Zealand waters

Maritime Rules Part 22: the New Zealand rules of the road.

  • Part 22 New Zealand waters, at sea and on lakes and rivers.

From the Almanac in Steady As, the instrument beside the helm for Apple Watch and iPhone. Steady As is not a government body. Check the official source before you rely on a rule.
