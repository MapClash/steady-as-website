Clause 14 Approval

Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

The construction of lights and shapes and the installation of lights on board the vessel must be to the satisfaction of—

(a) in the case of a New Zealand ship, the Director; or

(b) in the case of a foreign ship, the State whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly.
