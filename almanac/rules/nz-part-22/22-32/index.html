22.32 Definitions Relating to This Section

Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

In this section—

prolonged blast means a blast of from four to six seconds duration:

short blast means a blast of about one second’s duration:

whistle means any sound signalling appliance capable of producing the prescribed blasts and which complies with the specifications in Appendix 3 to this rule.
