22.28 Vessels constrained by their draught

Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

A vessel constrained by its draught may, in addition to the lights prescribed for power-driven vessels in rule 22.23, exhibit where they can best be seen, three all-round red lights in a vertical line or a black cylinder.
