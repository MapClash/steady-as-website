Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

Part 22 · New Zealand waters, at sea and on lakes and rivers.

The Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention, the rules for every vessel in New Zealand waters, at sea and on lakes and rivers, and New Zealand boats anywhere, consolidated to 31 March 2021. The official text, in English. New Zealand's own wording of the COLREGs, as consolidated by Maritime NZ; the signed rules are on its website.

Official text: maritimenz.govt.nz ›
