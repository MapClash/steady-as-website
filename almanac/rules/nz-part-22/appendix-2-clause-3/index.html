Clause 3 Signals for purse seiners

Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

Vessels engaged in fishing with purse seine gear may exhibit 2 yellow flashing lights in a vertical line. These lights must flash alternately every second and with equal light and occultation duration. These lights must only be exhibited when the vessel is hampered by its fishing gear.
