Clause 8 Intensity of lights

Maritime Rules Part 22: Collision Prevention

(1) The minimum luminous intensity of lights must be calculated by using the formula—

I = 3.43 × 10⁶ × T × D² × K⁻ᴰ

Where

I is luminous intensity in candelas under service conditions; and

T

is the threshold factor 2 x 10-7 lux;

D

is the range of visibility (luminous range) of the light in nautical miles; and

K

is atmospheric transmissivity.

For prescribed lights, the value of K must be 0.8, corresponding to a meteorological visibility of approximately 13 nautical miles.
