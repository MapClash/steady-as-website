Section 125 Surrender of authority

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) The holder of an authority may surrender it by written notice given to the administering agency.

(2) The notice must be accompanied by the document evidencing the authority unless—

(a) the authority is the registration of a Queensland regulated ship; and

(b) the document evidencing the authority was issued in the way provided for under section 159A.

(3) The surrender of the authority takes effect on—

(a) the day the notice is given; or

(b) if a later day is stated in the notice—the later day.
