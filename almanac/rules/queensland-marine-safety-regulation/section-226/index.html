Section 226 Statements about positive flotation taken to be statements about level flotation

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

A statement in the approved form for a ship that the ship has positive flotation made under a provision of the 2004 regulation mentioned in column 1 is, from the commencement, taken to be a statement in the approved form for the ship that the ship has level flotation made under the provision of this regulation mentioned in column 2—
