Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

Marine Safety Regulation · Queensland waters.

The Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016, the rules for boats in Queensland waters: safety equipment and lifejackets, licences and speed limits, current as at 1 February 2026. The full text, in English, from legislation.qld.gov.au. The version at the source is the one that counts. Queensland only: every Australian state has its own rules, in Local rules. Due to expire on 31 August 2027 under Queensland's staged repeal, when a new regulation takes its place.

Official text: legislation.qld.gov.au ›
