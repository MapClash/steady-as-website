Section 27 Requirement to register

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) A Queensland regulated ship must be registered as—

(a) if the ship is a recreational ship—a recreational ship; or

(b) if the ship is an other Queensland regulated ship—an other Queensland regulated ship.

(2) The owner of a Queensland regulated ship must ensure the ship is registered as mentioned in subsection (1) unless the owner has a reasonable excuse.

Maximum penalty for subsection (2)—200 penalty units.

Note— The procedure for registration of Queensland regulated ships is in chapter 6, part 2.
