Section 239 Application of s 32 during transitional period

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

For section 32(2), during the transitional period a builders plate fixed to a ship in accordance with the former standard is taken to be fixed to the ship in accordance with the ABP Standard.
