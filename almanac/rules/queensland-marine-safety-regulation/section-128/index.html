Section 128 Grant of marine licence on conditions

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) The administering agency may grant a marine licence on conditions the administering agency considers reasonable and relevant.

1 Under section 131, the marine licence indicator for a marine licence must indicate all conditions imposed on the licence under this regulation.

2 Part 16 of the Act provides for the review of a decision made by the administering agency under this section.

(2) If the administering agency imposes a condition on a marine licence, the administering agency must give the holder of the licence written notice of the condition.

(3) If the administering agency imposes a condition on a marine licence, the holder of the licence must not operate a ship in contravention of the condition.

Maximum penalty for subsection (3)—20 penalty units.
