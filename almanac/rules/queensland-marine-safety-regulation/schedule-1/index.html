Schedule 1 Coastal bars

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

Column 1

Column 2

Coastal Bar

Description

Bribie Island Bar

the passage of water between the northern and southern parts of Bribie Island in the vicinity of where Pumicestone Channel (commonly known as Pumicestone Passage) and the Coral Sea meet

Currumbin Bar

the bar located near the town of Currumbin in the vicinity of where the Currumbin Creek and the Coral Sea meet

Tallebudgera Bar

the bar located near the town of Burleigh Heads in the vicinity of where the Tallebudgera Creek and the Coral Sea meet

Gold Coast Seaway Bar

the passage of water between Nerang Head and Porpoise Point on South Stradbroke Island (commonly known as the Gold Coast Seaway)

Jumpinpin Bar

the passage of water between North Stradbroke Island and South Stradbroke Island

South Passage Bar

the passage of water between North Stradbroke Island and Moreton Island

Caloundra Bar

the bar located near the town of Caloundra in the vicinity of where the northern end of Pumicestone Channel (commonly known as Pumicestone Passage) and the Coral Sea meet

Mooloolah Bar

the bar located near Point Cartwright in the vicinity of where the Mooloolah River and the Coral Sea meet

Maroochy Bar

the bar located near the town of Maroochydore in the vicinity of where the Maroochy River and the Coral Sea meet

Noosa Bar

the bar located near the town of Noosa Heads in the vicinity of where the Noosa River and the Coral Sea meet

Wide Bay Bar

the bar located between the southern end of Fraser Island and Inskip Point in the vicinity of where the southern end of Great Sandy Strait and the Coral Sea meet

Round Hill Creek Bar

the bar located near the town of Seventeen Seventy in the vicinity of where Round Hill Creek and the Coral Sea meet

Cawarral Creek Bar

the bar located near the town of Keppel Sands in the vicinity of where the Cawarral Creek and the Coral Sea meet
