Section 213 Where register of division 3 undertakings must be kept

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

For section 183L of the Act, the place where the register of division 3 undertakings is available for public inspection is the office of the general manager.

Note— The address for the general manager is available on MSQ’s website.
