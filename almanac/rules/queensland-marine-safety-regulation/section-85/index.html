Section 85 Interfering with speed sign

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) A person must not interfere with a speed sign erected or marked by the general manager under section 206A(4) of the Act, or by the Gold Coast Waterways Authority under section 206AA(4) of the Act, unless the person has a reasonable excuse.

Maximum penalty—200 penalty units.

(2) In this section—

interfere with includes damage, destroy, mark and remove.
