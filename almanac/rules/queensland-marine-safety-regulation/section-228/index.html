Section 228 Requirement to keep particular documents about accreditation continues

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) This section applies if an accredited person carried on a business of ship designing, ship building or marine surveying, and brought into existence an operational plan or production record under former section 50.

(2) Former section 50(6)(c) continues to apply to the accredited person as if the 2004 regulation were still in force.

Note— Former section 50(6)(c) provides for the documents to be kept for at least 7 years after they are made.

(3) In this section—

accredited person means a person who was accredited as a ship designer, ship builder or marine surveyor under former section 44.
