Section 190 Buoy mooring to display identifying number

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

The holder of a buoy mooring approval for a buoy mooring must ensure—

(a) the identifying number issued by the approving entity for the buoy mooring is permanently and legibly displayed on the buoy; and

(b) the buoy and its identifying number are not obscured from view.

Maximum penalty—20 penalty units.
