Section 105 Grant of authority on conditions

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

The administering agency may grant an authority on conditions the administering agency considers reasonable and relevant.

1 Under section 107, the document evidencing an authority must state all conditions imposed on the authority under this regulation.

2 Part 16 of the Act provides for the review of a decision made by the administering agency under this section.

3 For consequences for a contravention of a condition of an authority, see—

• section 57(4) of the Act in relation to the registration of a ship

• section 54 in relation to restricted use authorities

• section 75(5) in relation to temporary permits

• sections 110(2) and 115 in relation to all authorities

• section 198(4) in relation to aquatic event authorities.
