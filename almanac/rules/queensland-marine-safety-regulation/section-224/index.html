Section 224 Registered EPIRB under 2004 regulation taken to have up-to-date registration

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) An EPIRB registered under former section 9B immediately before the commencement is, from the commencement, taken to be registered under section 10(2) of this regulation.

(2) If, immediately before the commencement, the EPIRB had up-to-date registration within the meaning of former section 9A, the EPIRB is, at the commencement, taken to have up-to-date registration under section 10 of this regulation.
