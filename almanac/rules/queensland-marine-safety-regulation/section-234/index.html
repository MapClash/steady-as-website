Section 234 Copy of former authorities may be given to person with interest in ship

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

(1) If a person obtains an interest in a ship, the person may apply to the general manager for a copy of any of the following issued under the 2004 regulation for the ship (each a former authority)—

(a) a certificate of survey;

(b) a load line certificate;

(c) a design approval certificate.

(2) The application must be in the approved form.

(3) The general manager may give a copy of the former authority to the person if the general manager is satisfied the person has an interest in the ship.

(4) The general manager may change the former authority before giving a copy of it to the person to ensure that the authority is addressed to the person.
