Section 157 Notice about application for removal of disqualification—Act, s 202L

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

For section 202L(4) of the Act, the written notice must be given to the chief executive—

(a) in the approved form; and

(b) at the address stated in the approved form as the address for giving the notice.
