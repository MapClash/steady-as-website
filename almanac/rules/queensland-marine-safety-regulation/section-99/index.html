Section 99 Who is an administering agency

Transport Operations (Marine Safety) Regulation 2016

The administering agency is—

(a) for an authority—the entity who may grant the authority under this regulation; or

(b) for a marine licence—the entity who may grant the marine licence under this regulation; or

(c) for a marine licence indicator—the entity who, under this regulation, may grant the marine licence to which the indicator relates.
