Règlement sur les abordages

Règlement sur les abordages · The same rules, in French.

The Règlement sur les abordages, the rules for every boat in Canadian waters and Canadian vessels anywhere: the International Rules with Canada's own changes, headed Canadian Modification, last amended on 7 June 2023. The official text, in French. The same rules in English are the Collision Regulations; both languages are official.

Official text: laws-lois.justice.gc.ca ›
