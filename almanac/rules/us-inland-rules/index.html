Inland Navigation Rules

Inland Rules · US inland waters, inside the COLREGS demarcation lines.

The Inland Navigation Rules, the rules on US inland waters, inside the COLREGS demarcation lines, current as of 22 October 2026. The official text, in English. Seaward of those lines the International Rules (COLREGs) apply.

Rule 1(g), § 83.01: a copy on board every self-propelled vessel of 12 m (39.4 ft) or more.
