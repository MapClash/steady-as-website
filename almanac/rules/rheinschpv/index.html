Rheinschiffahrtspolizeiverordnung

RheinSchPV · The Rhine from Basel to the sea.

The Rheinschiffahrtspolizeiverordnung, the rules on the Rhine from Basel to the sea, as published on 12 August 2025. The official text, in German. In the Netherlands the same rules are the RPR, in Dutch.

§ 1.11: small craft (Kleinfahrzeuge) need not carry a copy; it is here for reference.
