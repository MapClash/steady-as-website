Collision Regulations

Collision Regulations · Canadian waters, in English: the International Rules with Canada's own changes.

The Collision Regulations, the rules for every boat in Canadian waters and Canadian vessels anywhere: the International Rules with Canada's own changes, headed Canadian Modification, last amended on 7 June 2023. The official text, in English. The same rules in French are the Règlement sur les abordages; both languages are official.
