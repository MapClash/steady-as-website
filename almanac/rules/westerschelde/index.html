Scheepvaartreglement Westerschelde 1990

Westerschelde · The Westerschelde and its mouths.

The Scheepvaartreglement Westerschelde 1990, the rules on the Westerschelde and its mouths, with the sea off Vlissingen, in force from 1 January 2017. The official text, in Dutch.

Article 55: an up-to-date copy and an up-to-date chart of the area on board, on paper or a screen, except on a small open boat.
