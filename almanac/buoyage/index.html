Buoyage

Buoys and beacons, IALA A and B: which side to pass.

Buoys and beacons as the IALA system lays them out. Coming in from sea means into a harbor, up a river, or clockwise round a continent; a chart shows the direction where it isn't clear.

IALA A lateral marks

Europe, Africa, Asia except Japan, Korea and the Philippines, Australia and New Zealand. Coming in from sea: red to port, green to starboard.

  • PortKeep it on your port side coming in from sea.Light: R, any rhythm
  • StarboardKeep it on your starboard side coming in from sea.Light: G, any rhythm
  • Preferred channel to starboardA fork: the main channel is to starboard. Pass it as a port mark.Light: Fl(2+1) R
  • Preferred channel to portA fork: the main channel is to port. Pass it as a starboard mark.Light: Fl(2+1) G

IALA B lateral marks

North, Central and South America, Japan, South Korea and the Philippines. Coming in from sea: red to starboard, "red right returning".

  • PortKeep it on your port side coming in from sea.Light: G, any rhythm
  • StarboardKeep it on your starboard side coming in from sea.Light: R, any rhythm
  • Preferred channel to starboardA fork: the main channel is to starboard. Pass it as a port mark.Light: Fl(2+1) G
  • Preferred channel to portA fork: the main channel is to port. Pass it as a starboard mark.Light: Fl(2+1) R

Cardinal marks

The flashes follow a clock face: 3 at east, 6 at south, 9 at west. The cones point where the black is.

  • North cardinalSafe water to the north. Pass north of it.Light: Q or VQ, white, without a break
  • East cardinalSafe water to the east. Pass east of it.Light: Q(3) 10 s or VQ(3) 5 s, white
  • South cardinalSafe water to the south. Pass south of it.Light: Q(6)+LFl 15 s or VQ(6)+LFl 10 s, white
  • West cardinalSafe water to the west. Pass west of it.Light: Q(9) 15 s or VQ(9) 10 s, white

Other marks

  • Isolated dangerA small danger with safe water all round it. Give it room.Light: Fl(2), white
  • Safe waterSafe water all round: a landfall or the middle of a channel.Light: Iso, Oc, LFl 10 s or Morse A, white
  • Special markNot for navigation: a zone, a cable, a racing or bathing area. The chart says which.Light: Yellow, any rhythm
  • Emergency wreckA new wreck, not on the charts yet. Keep well clear.Light: Blue and yellow, alternating

Light rhythms

  • Fl
    Flashing: shorter on than off
  • Fl(2)
    Groups of two flashes
  • Q · VQ
    Quick, very quick: 60 and 120 flashes a minute
  • LFl
    One long flash, 2 seconds or more
  • Iso
    On and off for equal times
  • Oc
    Occulting: longer on than off
  • Al
    Alternating colors
  • 10 s
    The time for one whole cycle
