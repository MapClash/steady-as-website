South cardinal

Cardinal marks

Safe water to the south. Pass south of it.

Light: Q(6)+LFl 15 s or VQ(6)+LFl 10 s, white

The flashes follow a clock face: 3 at east, 6 at south, 9 at west. The cones point where the black is.
