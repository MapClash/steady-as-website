Canadian waters

The Coast Guard (MCTS) listens on 16 and 70, not on working channels.

  • 16
    Distress, urgency, safety and calling. Call the Coast Guard here first.
  • 70
    DSC only, no voice.
  • 68
    The general channel for recreational boaters.
  • 69 · 72
    Pleasure craft on the BC coast.
  • 71
    Marinas and yacht clubs, East Coast and Lake Winnipeg.
  • 21B · 83B · WX
    Weather and marine broadcasts, receive only.

78A is not a pleasure craft channel in Canada.

In port, turn the power down to 1 W. Transmitting needs the ROC-M.

Working channels differ by region; MCTS and VTS channels are in Radio Aids to Marine Navigation.

Source: Canadian Coast Guard, Radio Aids to Marine Navigation; ISED RBR-2; Transport Canada TP 511.

Checked against the official sources on 25 October 2026. Channels change: the official notice wins over this page.
