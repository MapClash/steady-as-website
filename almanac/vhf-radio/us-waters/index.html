US waters

Set the radio to the US channel plan. Some radios show 22A as 1022.

  • 16
    Distress, safety and calling. The Coast Guard keeps watch.
  • 9
    Boater calling.
  • 22A
    Coast Guard liaison and safety broadcasts, announced on 16.
  • 13
    Bridge to bridge, at 1 W. Ships of 20 m and more listen.
  • 68 · 69 · 71 · 78A
    Recreational working channels.
  • 72
    Recreational, ship to ship only.
  • WX1–WX7
    NOAA weather.

VTS, port and bridge channels are on the chart and in the Coast Pilot.

Source: US Coast Guard Navigation Center; 47 CFR 80.373.

Checked against the official sources on 25 October 2026. Channels change: the official notice wins over this page.
